**Compendiums**

- Two Actors compendiums, one for HârnMaster 3 and one for Song of Heroic Lands,
  each holding every NPC ready to drop onto a scene.
- Every NPC's appearance and dossier is a Journals entry as well as part of the
  actor, and the affiliations of Friends, Foes and Followers ship as Song of
  Heroic Lands items.
- Everything sits inside one Hârn Ensemble folder, and each compendium files its
  NPCs under the same two roots, Extras and Friends, Foes and Followers.
- Extras holds Clergy, Guards, Guilded, Mages, Nobles, Serfs, Thraals and Slaves,
  and Unguilded; Friends, Foes and Followers holds Barbarians, Clergy, Guilded,
  Mages, Military, Nobles, Non-Humans and Unguilded.
- The folders keep the identities they had, so a world that already imported
  them goes on finding them.
