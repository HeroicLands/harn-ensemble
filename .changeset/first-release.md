Requires Foundry VTT 14, verified against HârnMaster 3 1.6.5 and Song of Heroic Lands 0.8.5.

**Compendiums**

- Two Actor compendiums — one for HârnMaster 3, one for Song of Heroic Lands — each holding every NPC ready to drop onto a scene.
- Every NPC's appearance and dossier is a journal entry as well as part of the actor.
- Affiliations of Friends, Foes and Followers ship as Song of Heroic Lands items.
- NPCs are filed under Extras and Friends, Foes, Followers folders, with sunsign and movement rate displayed on HârnMaster 3 sheets.

**Website**

- The module page at www.heroiclands.org/harnensemble/ carries the manifest URL, documentation, and every NPC and affiliation with its own page.
