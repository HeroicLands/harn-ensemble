# harn-ensemble

## 0.0.1

### Patch Changes

The first release of Hârn Ensemble: ready-made Hârn NPCs for Foundry VTT 14, under HârnMaster 3 (verified against 1.6.5) or Song of Heroic Lands (verified against 0.8.8). A world loads the compendiums for whichever system it runs.

**Compendiums**

- Two Actor compendiums, one for HârnMaster 3 and one for Song of Heroic Lands, each holding every NPC ready to drop onto a scene.
- Every NPC says whether it is a character or an NPC, and lists the archetypes it fits, so you can tell at a glance what a person is good for.
- Every NPC's appearance and dossier is a journal entry as well as part of the actor, and its journal links skills and mystical abilities to their descriptions.
- The affiliations of Friends, Foes and Followers ship as Song of Heroic Lands items.
- Everything sits in one Hârn Ensemble folder. Extras holds Clergy, Guards, Guilded, Mages, Nobles, Serfs, Thraals and Slaves, and Unguilded; Friends, Foes and Followers holds Barbarians, Clergy, Guilded, Mages, Military, Nobles, Non-Humans and Unguilded.
- Under HârnMaster 3, an NPC's sheet shows its sunsign and movement rate; under Song of Heroic Lands, a character speaks and writes every language and script their record states.

**Website**

- The module's page is at [www.heroiclands.org/harnensemble/](https://www.heroiclands.org/harnensemble/): what it is, the manifest URL to paste into Foundry, and the two systems it serves.
- Every NPC and every affiliation has its own page, characters link to descriptions of their folk, and every page lists what links to it and what it links to.
- A search box covers every page's text and can be narrowed by page type.
