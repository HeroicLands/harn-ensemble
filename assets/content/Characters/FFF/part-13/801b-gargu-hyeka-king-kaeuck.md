## Biography

The gargun of the Sorkin Mountains that border the Kathela Hills have a love/hate relationship with the indigenous Kath, meaning they love to hate their human neighbors.

Kaeuck's colony makes frequent raids to despoil Kath camps and capture prisoners. Those Kath taken alive quickly envy the dead when Kaeuck and his warriors start their "sport." A prisoner who dies early in the orgy of raping, killing, and eating (not necessary in that order) is deemed fortunate indeed by those who otherwise suffer unimaginably before they meet their end. One of Kaeuck's favorite entertainments involves seeing how long it takes a prisoner to die when a sharpened roasting spit is slowly pushed up through the anus and out the mouth. He decorates himself with totems of human hair and hands taken from prisoners he has personally "sported" with.

Kaeuck took the kingship of his colony when he buried his mankar in the king's head while the king was in congress with the queen. It is a testament to Kaeuck's craftiness that he was able to bribe the guards of the queen's chamber. He has since held the kingship for a solid year and seems likely to hold it for a few more while he keeps his warriors busy raiding their human neighbors rather than idling and spending time plotting his demise.

Kaeuck's constant warfare with the Kath recently reached a peak when his warriors ambushed a hunting party and carried away Kesta, the beautiful and greatly beloved daughter of a powerful Kath chieftan, Paewe. A band of Kath warriors was sent to rescue Kesta but many moons have passed and they have not returned. Paewe has sworn to retrieve his daughter no matter the cost.
