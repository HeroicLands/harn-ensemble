## Biography

Margum is king of a Gargu-viasal hive that has been at war with Azadmere since shortly after the fall of Lothrim's empire. A dreaded warrior, he is a cunning tactician who has gathered an impressive list of victo- ries over his foes and gained a corresponding number of blood-oaths to take his head in vengeance. Few gar- gun have received as much notoriety amongst the Khuzdul. Margum's own warriors have a healthy fear of their king and will not raise a weapon against him, as those who have experienced the power of his axe first- hand have not survived it.

Margum gained kingship in typical gargun fashion, by killing the previous king in mortal combat before the assembled warriors. In celebration of his victory, Mar- gum led his warriors in a raid on hated Azadmere and overcame a caravan of human and Khuzdul merchants. Rather than condemning the captives to the roasting pit, Margum ordered them tortured within earshot of well-traveled routes and kygs. The prisoners' screams and pleas for mercy drew out a band of honor-bound Khuzdul to rescue their aggrieved kinsmen but arrows and falling rocks doomed the would-be rescuers. Among the fallen was a favored kinsman of the Khuzdul king, a brash youth short of beard. From this slain war- rior, Margum stole the Axe of Aluot, a treasured Khuzan artifact, an act that has caused great grief among the Khuzdul. Attempts by the King of Azadmere to retrieve the axe have consistently failed due to Margum's crafti- ness and skill.

Margum has garnered an impressive collection of artifacts from his many foes. His prized possessions in- clude a suit of stout Khuzan scale armor fitted to his frame, a shield, and his favorite weapon, the masterfully crafted Axe of Aluot (see artifact article). He replenishes his kilt of Khuzdul beards every year.

HOOKS

Scalp Hunting. The PCs are set upon by Margum's gargun looking for loot and captives. One of the PCs is mistaken for a Khuzdul. Woe to this PC, as Margum rel- ishes collecting their beards and scalps!

Vengeance! The PCs are hired by agents of the King of Azadmere as assassins to secretly hunt down Margum, kill him, and regain the Axe of Aluot. The king hopes that a knife in the dark will succeed where overt military might has continuously failed.

To the Rescue! Those defeated in battle by Mar- gum's hive do not always suffer immediate death. The cruel king often uses captives as bait to draw out his foes. The PCs are anxious to rescue a companion be- fore he suffers a hideous fate. But is this a trap?
