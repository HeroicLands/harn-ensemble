## Biography

Jamar is a stranger in a strange land. He was born in Hacherdad, a Grand Principality far, far from Harn. The fourth son of a perfumer, he should have stayed and worked for his eldest brother. This all changed 16 years ago. During a transaction, the buyer asked if he could pay with a strange gold coin from Azadmere, a Dwarven kingdom on an island far to the northwest. The instant Jamar's hand touched the coin, his life changed.

Touching the coin awoke a power that Jamar didn't even know he had. He had a vision of a misty isle and a vague premonition that he was fated to go there. He tried to put the idea out of his mind but it haunted his thoughts and consumed his dreams. He could not escape it. Jamar sought out mystics from Hacherdad's Chantry of Mysteries. They revealed that Jamar was the inheritor of great gifts, those of prescience and psychometry. He trained with the Chantry for three years to control the visions, but they would not go away. Three years after the coin came into his life, he gave in and decided to follow his fate.

The trip to Harn was difficult and his life as an outsider is hard, but Jamar had no choice, the Misty Isle called him. Despite years of analyzing his dreams and travelling all over Harn, he is no closer to finding the meaning of the riddle. The best he can make out is that there is someone on Harn who has handled the coin and who is key to his future. He moves every few years, looking for the elusive person in his dreams. During this time, he has gained more control over his gifts and can sometimes use them consciously.

## Adventure Hooks

Stunned Silence. One of the PCs goes to the new perfumer in town to buy a bottle of perfume. The man is apparently the finest perfumer around, despite the fact he is strange and exotic and speaks with a foreign accent. When the PC enters, the man looks at him with a combination of fear, horror, and delight. "It's you!"

I Will Stop You. One of the PCs bumps into a man in the street. As the man touches the PCs hand, he recoils in horror. He points at them and says, "I know what you are going to do! I won't let it happen." The man becomes a constant nuisance for no reason.

My Master. After encountering the PCs, the perfumer asks (begs) to be their servant. "I have foreseen it; you will lead me to my destiny." Despite his puzzling introduction, he proves to be a loyal servant, cook, and guard. His predictions can be uncanny.
