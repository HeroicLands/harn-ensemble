## Biography

Sarey's older sister Elderien holds a guild franchise i Cherafir. They both studied under their grandfather after their parents died of the cough but Elderien was the studious and attentive one. Sarey has the ability to be a very good glassmaker, if only he would apply himself. However, he preferred to sneak off to go fishing or make dinner instead. His grandfather frequently beat him in hopes it would make him more of a man. It did not; all it did was make him more and more determined to get away as soon as possible.

As soon as he earned his journeyman credentials, Sarey packed up his bags and bid farewell to Cherafir. He has been travelling Harn ever since. He picks up work with local glassworkers here and there for a day, a week, or a month. Sarey stays until the local master gets tired of his laziness and sends him on his way. More than once, his skills at fishing and cooking have kept him fed or even earned him a place in a caravan or a few nights accommodation. He did train with the militia in Cherafir and carries a half helm, shield, and short sword, but he is not very good with them. He is fat and lazy, neither of which make a good swordsman.

Sarey drifted into town last month with a small caravan. He found work with a local glassworker. His new master sees his potential and is trying to figure out how to harness his pig-headedness to the art of glassblowing. Sarey actually likes the man and has been working harder then usual to master the skill of blowing glass. Who knows, he might actually stick around. It helps that he has met a local girl who doesn't mind his soft exterior as long as his pulse is full.

## Adventure Hooks

Friendly Patron: The PCs meet Sarey in the local tavern. He is a friendly and gregarious young man, if a bit overweight, with red hair and intense blue eyes. Also new to the town, he is much less standoffish than the locals and is willing to talk. Sarey proves to be a good source of information about the community.

That's My Girl: The PCs blow into town, romantic adventurers with full purses. Sarey's girlfriend decides tha these hard, muscular men are more to her liking and starts to flirt with the male party members. Sarey is livid and bad things start to happen to the PCs.

Going My Way? As the PCs prepare to leave town, a heavyset young journeyman looking for company on the road approaches them. He is a good cook and will work as their servant if he can join their party.
