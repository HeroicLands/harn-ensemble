## Biography

Peri spent most of his life as a farmer. He and his wife and children lived a reasonably comfortable life farming the land he inherited from his father. Six years ago, Peri's plow got stuck on a large rock in the soil. While trying to get the team of oxen to step back and relieve the pressure on the plow, his attention was diverted and one of the oxen stepped on his right foot. It was crushed instantly by the full weight of the ox. The wound later turned septic and he nearly died. Only the skill of the village's Peonian priestess saved him. The year it took to recover cost him his farm; his wife had to sell it to her brother to cover their expenses during the full year he couldn't work.

After he recovered, Peri was desperate for work. He could walk, slowly and with a limp, but not for long distances. Fortunately, he was good friends with the village woodcrafter, a kindly man with no children and no apprentice. After some prodding from the priestess, the woodcrafter agreed to take Peri as his apprentice. Already 36 years old and crippled, it was hard for Peri to accept, but he had to think of his family. They couldn't live on his brother-in-law's charity forever.

Recently promoted to journeyman, Peri is, at best, an indifferent woodcrafter. He doesn't have a feel for the wood. He produces adequate work but nothing special. If he were a youth, the old master would have turned him out long ago. However, he sympathizes with Peri and wants to help him. He has done his best to teach Peri enough to get by. Peri works better with the metal pieces he makes for hinges, hooks, latches, etc.

## Adventure Hooks

Good Hearted Neighbour: The PCs take up residence next to Peri. He works long hours because he moves slowly. He is cheerful and friendly and always willing to lend a hand. He volunteers to keep an eye on the PCs residence when they are away.

I Have a Family to Support. The PCs need some woodwork done and hire Peri. The job is very poorly done. They complain and refuse to pay. Under the cheerful exterior is a nasty and bitter man. Peri begins a campaign of lies and rumours to ruin the PCs.

Do You Have a Horse? The PCs need some work done quietly. One of their neighbours recommends Peri. He is not the best woodcrafter, but he is poor, desperate, and willing to do almost anything for coin. The only limitation is that if it is any distance, he will need a ride.
