## Biography

Esharel (Sha for short) is a Jarin from Arone. His father died when he was young. His mother, a cattle herder, raised him alone. In 700TR, while Sha was out tending cattle, Jarin rebels raided Arone, killing two huscarls. In retribution, the Ivinians hung twenty Jarin peasants, including his mother. Nursing a burning desire for revenge, Sha fled into the wilderness and joined the rebels. There, he met Zoe, beginning a life long friendship.

Sha rose quickly among the rebels. His natural leadership ability got him promoted to sergeant, with Zoe as his corporal. In 701TR, he helped plan and lead the liberation of Lorkin Castle. From 701-703TR, the 'Independent Years,' he fought against the hated Ivinians. In 703TR, the Jarin leader, Gweffryn Seylnes, was mysteriously decapitated. Sha held the men together for two more weeks before the castle fell to Clan Geldestaar. Sha and Zoe slipped over the wall and across the Anoth River.

A witty, bitter and angry man, Sha lost more than the war; he lost his sense of compassion and forgiveness and all faith in the Jarin lords and Siem. There are now very few people he trusts. Sha relations with Ivinians are hostile at best. Sha bristles at the term Orbaal, referring to i as Occupied Jara and its overlords as Ivinians.

After the defeat at Lorkin, Sha and Zoe crossed over to Lythia, fighting as mercenaries for Trierzon against Shorkyne. In 713TR, on the Plains of Karetan, Sha ad the captured Baron of Suvas, Sir Dasen Queridona a wealthy liegeman of Clan Pelanby. He ransomed him for L120. Sha poured the ransom and the remains of his heart and soul into the purchase of a dilapidated dak, renaming her Lorkin. With the minimum repairs he set sail, free at last.

In addition to legitimate transport runs, Sha engages in what he likes to call "perfectly legitimate entrepreneurial endeavors". No job is too big, too small, or too illegal, as long as it keeps his crew together and his ship sailing. Captain Ranals is very protective of his crew and lives by a very strong moral code centred on his word, his loyalty to his crew and an inconvenient sense of charity.
