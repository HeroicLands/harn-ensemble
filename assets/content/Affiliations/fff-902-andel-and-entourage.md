On Harn, a man is judged by the size of his entourage. The number of staff, servants and guards is a measure of his power, status and position. Having too many retainers is presumptuous and may (in extreme cases) even be considered a breach of rank, however, too few retainers and an individual risks not being taken seriously. A lone clerk, arriving tired and dusty on the back of a donkey is hardly suitable for a member of the King's Household. Although a Clerk of the Exchequer, Andel is only a guildsman's son and he pushes the limit with the size of his retinue.

## MEMBERS

### EXCHEQUER CLERK: [FFF 510-1] Andel Gebar

A senior civil servant, Andel loves the power and prestige of his position. He is scrupulously polite and deferential to all knights and nobles, but at the same time makes it perfectly clear that he is the representative of the King. He is prone to use terms like "the King has made it clear that..." and "during my last audience with the King...." Despite the fact that he is really only a minor functionary, he does have considerable power, but it mainly consists of the ability to ensure certain documents and complaints reach the right ears and others simply disappear. He can be a pompous git when dealing with his servants, but they are used to it by now.

### SCRIBE: [FFF 415-1] Reld Drosyl

Reld is Andel's right hand man. His outstanding memory, encyclopaedic knowledge of records, decisions, legal precedents and previous inventories, makes him the perfect foil to Andel's schemes. A friendly and relaxed fellow, he is the most approachable member of the party, often circulating around the manor, talking to servants, farmhands and serfs, listening, watching and remembering everything. In audiences, he sits at Andel's right hand, taking detailed notes and occasionally leaning over to whisper an important detail in his ear.

### COOK: [FFF 606-1] Riatha Deherne

Riatha has been with Andel for six years. She has seen other servants and staff come and go, while Andel takes much of what she does for granted. His food is always cooked the way he likes; his clothes are washed and laid out for him and his chambers are always neat and tidy. In truth, he would be lost without her. She manages the daily finances of the group, purchasing food, supplies, paying the casual labour and planning the schedule. Andel gravely underestimates her intelligence; the other members of the entourage are not that foolish.

### MANSERVANT: [FFF 401-1] Dramin Naradas

An animal trainer by trade, Dramin works primarily as a manservant, carrying bags, cutting wood and fetching water. His real talent is with the animals, especially the mules. They respond well to him and he can get more out of them than anyone else. A skilled woodsman, he is a key member of the party when they camp in the wilderness. An excellent hunter and tracker, he is the only member of the party skilled with a bow.

### SENIOR MAN-AT-ARMS: [FFF 109-1] Petin Muldabel

A retired Thardic Legionnaire, Petin has been a mercenary since he was twenty. He cares little for politics and goes where the money is, selling his loyalty to the person who pays him the most. Petin has been with Andel nearly as long as Riatha, and this allows him some latitude. On issues of security, Andel defers to him. Although he always grumbles about the cost, he values his own skin more. The clerk has accumulated a number of enemies over the years. Petin knows most of them by sight and despite his relaxed attitude is always watching. He works well with the other servants and they follow his lead in times of danger.

### JUNIOR MAN-AT-ARMS: [FFF 112-1] Kernt Mondaro

The younger of the two men-at-arms, Kernt is a solid, dependable man-at-arms. He is skilled with weapons and takes his position seriously. A religious man (Laranian), he keeps his views to himself. He attends services whenever they are near a temple. Kernt is also a bit of a gambler. He likes a good game of chance and can often be found in the taverns in the evenings. Perhaps a better leader than Petin, Kernt differs to his greater experience and legion training.
