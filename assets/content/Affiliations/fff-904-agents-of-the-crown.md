This company of adventurers represent the long arm of the crown and courts in Melderyni (or any other feudal kingdom). Answerable directly to the King through the to the Inquisitor-General and Chancellor, a Royal Agent is a man to be feared and respected. If you are honest and have committed no crime, you have nothing to fear.

## Members

### ROYAL AGENT: [FFF 510-2] Sir Jorn Stury
Born at Nejir Manor, just outside of Zuilos Keep on the mainland of Melderyn, Sir Jorn is a second son. His older brother, Sir Waesle, is a much milder character preferring farming and the manor to campaigning. With little prospect for an inheritance, Sir Jorn took to fighting. He fought valiantly defending Thay from the Ivinians in 705TR. It was there, he hired his loyal retainer, Gwyn. After the war, with little hope for substantial employment and no desire to live off his brother's charity, he left for Trierzon. There, he and Gwyn served in the great Laranian army fighting the heathen Azeryan Empire.

Sir Jorn amassed a large nest egg and returned to Thay to retire. He bought a comfortable townhouse and invested his money with a local mercantyler, which provides him with a very comfortable living. His longsuffering wife, left behind for many years while he was abroad, pushed him into accepting this position of Royal Agent for Lenedshire in hopes it would win her power and prestige. The couple do not get along; he prefers his mistress, the proprietor of the local tavern, "The Bush", or a quick roll in the hay with his cook/housekeeper.

Despite being henpecked into taking the job, Sir Jorn takes his duties very seriously. He will investigate all crimes in his jurisdiction to the best of his abilities, no matter whose toes he steps on. This has made him popular with the chancellor and very unpopular with the local nobility.

### DEPUTY, SQUIRE & BODYGUARD: [FFF 127-2] Gwyn of Polruan
The son of a freeman, Gwyn was a fisherman and a slaughter man, but for the last fifteen years he has been Sir Jorn's squire/body servant, bodyguard, henchman, companion and friend. Most recently, he has also been unofficial deputy Royal Agent. Referred to as the "Ginger Giant" because of his reddish hair, height and massive build, there are very few people who are not intimidated by this gruff tree-trunk of a man. His huge body needs regular replenishment and so food and drink are always a high priority. He has proven a most able assistant in investigating several crimes.

In his travels, he has seen all the "Faces of Death" and so can quickly point out how a person died. Between he and the Agent, there is little they do not know about how to kill someone.

### DEFROCKED CLERIC & SCRIBE: [FFF 607-1] Toris of Peyne
A former Peonian cleric, Toris was defrocked and thrown out of the priesthood for supposedly raping a fourteen year old girl he was teaching to read and write. Toris was innocent, but that didn't matter to the girl's father or the church, which depended on her family's substantial donations. Shunned by his family, he spent several years trying to scratch out a living as a scribe. Finally, he threw himself on the mercy of his father's cousin, the High Priest at Chief Temple of Peoni on Harn, in Thay. Out of the goodness of his heart, his cousin found him a position with a local knight who had recently been appointed as Royal Agent. Toris job is to keep detailed records of the (nearly-illiterate) Agent's investigations. The records are later dispatched to Cherafir to the office of the Inquisitor-General.
