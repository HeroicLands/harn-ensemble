This company of adventurers consists of the small group of rogues and misfits that make up the officers and passengers aboard the Dak-class merchant vessel, Lorkin. Led by Captain Esharel Ranals, who fought on the losing side of the Jarin Rebellion against the Ivinian invaders, this eclectic crew makes a marginal living by taking transport jobs, legal or illegal, among the ports of Harn and western Lythia.

## Members

### THE CAPTAIN: [FFF 115-2] Esharel 'Sha' Ranals

Smuggler, thief, captain-for-hire, veteran of the Jarin Rebellion, Sha wants to be left alone by the Ivinians. Good at making clever plans; they never turn out the way he expects them to. He will steal, trick and kill, but his moral code dictates strict loyalty to his crew. He loves Kayly and Zoe like a brother, while his feelings for Inara are a mix of deep longing, respect and jealousy of her clients.

### THE FIRST MATE: [FFF 127-3] Zoe Alleyne

The toughest soldier on Harn, she has been Sha's loyal right hand since the war. Nothing surprises her; she's not afraid to challenge Sha when he's is wrong, but always backs his decisions. In his absence, she leads with a cool and steady hand. She is married to her beloved and romantic husband, Fior.

### THE PILOT: [FFF 325-2] Hoban 'Fior' Fiorfohd

Fior took the job of piloting the Lorkin because the work interested him. He is never more alive than when sailing the Lorkin on some crazy, impossibly dangerous course. Good humoured and easy going, he is no fighter, so on any job, his post is on the ship, ready for a quick getaway. Between jobs, he is always looking for a chance to take his wife out on a romantic date.

### THE MARINE: [FFF 115-3] Eryla Cobb

Eryla is a skilled, hardened and amoral killer. Ambitious, greedy and completely mercenary, he would sell out anyone (except his mom) for the right price. He is loyal to Sha, for now, but when the money is right "it'll be an interesting day." The crew of the Lorkin are the first group he has fall into that weren't horrible and it confuses him.

### THE CHIPPY: [FFF 329-2] Kayly Frye

A natural genius with ships, Kayly left her family without a moment's hesitation when Sha offered her the job as Lorkin's chippy. She's friendly and at home with everyone from cultured Inara, to coarse Eryla and crazy Kiba, accepting and loving them all. Lorkin is like her beloved child, whom she nurtures and praises. She is irrepressibly cheerful and optimistic and there isn't a soul on the ship who wouldn't give their life to protect her.

### THE CLERIC: [FFF 610-1] Derrial Parma

Parma came to Lorkin straight from Evael, wanting to travel the world a while. He is a devout and compassionate man who prays regularly and speaks up for peace and non-violence. He knows much more than a cleric should about violence, crime and all manner of trouble.

### THE COURTESAN: [FFF 307-1] Inara Serra

The entrancing professional courtesan's education included the social and sensual skills and graces and a few surprising things like dagger-combat. She left Trierzon for unknown reasons and now makes her living on the sea. Inara can make herself at home almost anywhere, from the King's court to a rowdy tavern. She rarely raises her voice or looses her temper, except with Sha. They play a game of not having feelings for each other.

### THE PHYSICIAN: [FFF 324-2] Llawaur Tam

The son of a rich family, Llawaur was a successful physician, until he discovered his sister, Kiba, was in trouble. He rescued her from the Agrikans, then fled to Melderyn and took passage on the Lorkin. Often lost and out of place on this rough frontier, his exquisite manners can be pretentious or laughable. His attraction for Kayly is hampered by shyness and knack for saying the wrong thing.

### THE FUGITIVE: [FFF 399-1] Kiba Tam

So smart she makes her brilliant brother look like an "idiot child," Kiba was a genius at everything from studies to dancing, until the Crimson Dancers enticed her into a special school and began messing with her mind. Though still brilliant, she is unstable and completely unpredictable. Inside, Kiba is still an insightful young girl who has a way of seeing the truth no matter how it is hidden.

## Lorkin as Serenity

Nine NPCs in FFF6, ACOA 3 and the 64 Foot Dak (Lorkin) are an interconnected set of articles about a seagoing adventure group. They are loosely based on the Joss Whedon Sci-Fi TV show **Firefly** and the movie **Serenity**. We have attempted to capture the fun and energy of this Sci-Fi show and translate it into a Harnic context.

They idea for a Harnic version of these people, places and adventures is based on the events of HarnCon III in Trenton, Ontario, July 2005. On the Friday evening, the group adjourned back to my place for an evening of visiting. During the evening, the subject of Firefly came up. I just happened to have a copy of the DVD boxed set and so we put in the pilot episode. Everyone loved it. Sunday morning, Andy Gibson was scheduled to run a session of "Prime Time Adventures", a game where the players are actors on a TV program. During Saturday, Ken Snellings, Andy Gibson and I discussed adapting Firefly (which we all knew and loved) as a Harnic TV show. We did so and Sunday morning's session was a blast.

After the game was finished, we talked about how cool it would be to do this in a more permanent form. So, the three of us decided to create FFF characters for each of the characters in Firefly, develop a back-story that paralleled Firefly (as much as possible, given Harnic constraints) and design a ship appropriate to the story. This download is the result. For those that are interested in the decisions we made, or don't know the series well enough to pick up the parallels, I have spelled them out explicitly below. The translations to the Harnic are courtesy of Ken Snellings, based on original language files from N. Robin Crossby himself.

Once all of these parallels were established, I wrote a background for the group. I hope you enjoy it.

### The Concepts

#### Alliance = Ivinians

The role of the 'oppressive enemy' was exceptionally painful to cast. In the original "Prime Time Adventures" scenario, it was the Council of Eleven, the Melderyni government and the Melderyni Royal Navy. That was problematic because it cast Sha and his crew in the role of survivors of the Capt Renda disaster. Ken was adamant that Ivinians were not a good match for Sha and crew.

Eventually after much discussion and input from Ken and Andy, I decided on the Ivinians as the powerful group, expanding their grip ever wider and putting more and more people under their thumb. Sha was cast as a Jarin freedom fighter ever trying to avoid their grasp.

It is an admitted stretch. Unlike Mal, Sha and crew could simply sail away to Trierzon and be done with it. They don't, because unlike in the Firefly universe, theJarin rebellion still simmers and is not completely extinguished. Sha has lost faith in the disparate rebel groups and spineless Jarin lords, but he stays close by, hoping that one day, his beloved Jara will rise again. I think it works sufficiently well for our purposes.

#### Independents = Jarin

Once the oppressive enemy was cast, the role of oppressed freedom loving folk was obvious.

#### Central Planets = Ivinian Kingdoms

The central planets (core worlds) of the Alliance portrayed in Firefly are recast as the various Ivinian kingdoms: Balisheim, Beshea, Eldeskaar, Gibenmark, Govyna, Ibanvaal\*, Jarenmark\*, Lokis, Lokemheim, Menglana\*, Rogna\*, Seldenbaal\*, Tavu, Chelemby, Harbaal, Palithane and Shorkyne (Alagon). The kingdoms marked with an asterisk (\*) are those who directly participated in the colonization of Free Jara (Orbaal). They are stretching their power ever outward, coveting more land and putting more people under the iron rule.

#### Outer Planets = Southern Harn

In this interpretation, the Ivinian Kingdoms are the core systems, spreading outward. Orbaal is as far as their grasp has reached. Mal/Sha keeps "just a little further" away by moving south to Melderyn, Kanday and Rethem.

#### Battle of Serenity Valley = Siege of Lorkin Castle

The Battle of Serenity Valley was the last battle of the Alliance - Independence Civil War. In the Firefly TV show, it is where Mal and Zoe fought their last battle of the war. The Siege of Lorkin Castle was the last battle of the Jarin Rebellion against the Ivinians in Orbaal; this is where Sha and Zoe fought in my version.

#### Serenity = Lorkin

Mal named his ship after his last battle so did Sha.

#### Firefly = Dak

A Dak was chosen for the Lorkin because we needed a ship big enough to carry the paying passengers and also large enough to handle the private crew cabins and great room that figured so prominently in the series.

#### Note on the Size of the Crew

Due to the lack of labour saving devices on the Lorkin, the ship requires a bigger crew than a Firefly class transport. I have addressed this by making the 'named' crew of the Serenity officers on the Lorkin, and by physically separating the sailors from the officers and passengers (the main NPCs). Ken and I have also decided to call them the 'red shirts' after the anonymous, interchangeable and easily replaceable red shirted extras on the original Star Trek series. They are there due to the requirements imposed by Pilot's Almanac, but the GM is free to ignore them from a narrative point of view.

#### Hands of Blue = Order of the Crimson Dancer

The ominous "Two by two, hands of blue" bad guys in Firefly are presumed to be some shadowy company (the Blue Sun Corporation?) within the Alliance. In our scenario, the role was given to the Agrikan Order of the Crimson Dancer, with the rhyme "Fear and dread, dance of red." The Crimson Dancer has a branch of their order in Ivinia (see page 1 of the Ivinia Index under the entry for Agrik), and their order has a temple in Kiba and Llawaur's city (see the entry for Beleka on the same page). The Crimson Dancers spotted Kiba by her aura and then sent her far away to their headquarters in distant Orbaal. They run the 'special school' that Kiba is taken to.

#### Reavers = Gargun/Foulspawn

Another imperfect fit, the gargun are not ocean going, but they do make a good enemy for the Jarin Resistance. The Ivinians are pushing out from the settlements, the gargun are pushing down from the mountains, and the resistance is caught in the middle.

### THE LOCATIONS

#### Central Planet: Londinium = Ivinia

It is the first of the two main core worlds/co-capitals. In Sha's world, it is the source of the oppressors of his people, the enemy.

#### Central Planet: Sihnon = Harbaal

It is the second of the two main core worlds/cocapitals. Few invaders came from Harbaal, but he still loathes them.

#### Central Planet: Ariel = Chelemby

An Ivinian Kingdom, Llawaur knows this sort of place well and thus was able to assist the crew in planning and executing one of their more successful heists here.

#### Central Planet: Osiris = Seldenbaal

This is where Simon and River grew up. Llawaur and Kiba grew up in Beleka, Seldenbaal.

#### Central Planet: Bellerophon = Palithane

An ocean retirement planet where the rich retire (in the Firefly universe), it is the kingdom where the rich Ivinians live on Lythia. Sha pulled a big job there, stealing an Earthmaster artefact (Lassiter laser in Mal's 'verse).

#### Outer Planet: Hera = Lorkin

This frontier planet was the site of the final battle of the Unification War, the Battle of Serenity Valley. In Orbaal, the site of the final battle of the Jarin rebellion was the Anoth Valley at Lorkin Castle.

#### Outer Planet: Shadow = Arone

This is where Mal was born and grew up. His mother was a relatively successful rancher. In the Harnic version, Sha grew up in Arone. After the Ivinians killed his mother, he fled into the hills and joined the rebels.

#### Outer Planet: Erza = Anoth Delta

This is the planet where Mal came to sell stolen drugs to a local fence. Niska's space station orbits this planet. In our scenario, the place is the Anoth Delta, the people he is selling to are Jarin Rebels and the bad guy is Esobran of Geltyne, the pirate (see below).

#### Outer Planet: Boros = Quimen

The home of the Order of the Crimson Dancer, the Agrikans have been ruthless with the Jarin serfs of the Quimen Valley since the collapse of the Jarin rebellion.

#### Outer Planet: Athens = Rethem

A frontier world with several moons, including Whitefall, strictly speaking this is not civilization.

#### Moon: Whitefall = Golotha

This is the place where Patience lives and Mal got shot. It is also home to Eryla's mother.

#### Moon: Higgins' Moon = Queste

This is the place where Jayne is worshiped as a Robin Hood figure.

#### Outer Planet: Persephone = Melderyn

While it is large and well settled, Persephone was still considered a frontier planet.

#### Place: Eavesdown Docks = Cherafir

This is the place where Llawaur, Kiba and Parma board the Lorkin.

#### Outer Planet: Beaumonde = Kanday

This is considered one of the better frontier planets.
