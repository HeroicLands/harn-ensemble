This company of adventurers represent the long arm of the crown and courts in Melderyni (or any other feudal kingdom). Answerable directly to the King through the to the Inquisitor-General and Chancellor, a Royal Agent is a man to be feared and respected. If you are honest and have committed no crime, you have nothing to fear.

## Members

### CLERIC OF SARAJIN: [FFF 608-2] Grimhilda Forsetha

Related to Baron Forsetha by blood, Grimhilda came to Harn seeking her destiny. She believes that Sarajin has a purpose for her on the Island; she just has not discovered it yet. Until she does, she is content to travel as Kjal's representative and study the lands and peoples of Harn. Trained at Kyriheim as a shieldmaiden and cleric of Sarajin, she feels that it is her duty to be a shining example to others of the strength and courage of Sarajin's followers.

### SCRIBE: [FFF 415-2] Ygwelve Forsetha

Ygwelve left behind a good and certain future for the great unknown of life on Harn and adventure with her sister Grimhilda. Since coming to Harn, she has had a number of strange episodes, seeing visions, some of which come to pass. Originally trained as a scribe, she has a good ear for languages. She has also proven to be skilled in the arts of diplomacy and negotiation, skills that her more martially inclined companions' lack. She is quietly amused, and somewhat intrigued, by Brynn's attempts to impress and attract her.

### KNIGHT BACHELOR: [FFF 506-4] Sir Chadrin Benere

A knight with an uncertain future until he gained employment with Baron Forsetha, Sir Benere is a formidable warrior with a great disdain for the petty politics of nobility. His strongest desire is to gain lands and title, not through heredity or politics, but the old fashioned way of earning them through the sword and shield. A member of the Laranian faith, he has made it clear that he has no interest in conversion to Sarajin, but he has developed a great respect for the Forsethas and other members of their faith.

### LONGBOWMAN: [FFF 127-5] Wyrin of Varross

The longbowmen of Chybisa have a well-deserved reputation as skilled archers, Wyrin stands out as exceptional amongst them. Born and raised in Geda, William is proud to serve as yeoman to the Baron. William has heard stories of the Sindarin and their skills at archery and bow making and hopes one day to have a contest against them. He would also like to see one of the famous Hart bows. A recent convert to Sarajin worship, Wyrin has become a good friend to Grimhilda, often serving as her squire.

### HODIRI HORSEMAN: [FFF 206-4] Brynn

A member of the Temhui tribe of the Hodiri, Brynn is a skilled warrior, horseman, and wilderness guide. His father had asked him to spend a few seasons living amongst the "Shiny Hat" people to study their ways. Kjal had employed him as a horse trainer for a year when Ygwelve Forsetha arrived. Brynn would have returned to his people by now, but the sight of Ygwelve changed his mind and captured his heart.

### MERCANTYLER: [FFF 318-2] Gaeffel of Thola

The son of the bonded weaponcrafter to Caer Geda, Gaeffel has a reputation for getting into trouble. Trained as a mercantyler in Thay, Gaeffel a more cosmopolitan view of the world than the rest of his companions. Gaeffel returned to Geda as a journeyman mercantyler under questionable circumstances and with little money. Seeing an opportunity, Kjal hired the young man as a bonded mercantyler. Gaeffel has served him well, uncovering some irregularities with the Anadel mines and locating trade goods of interest to the Baron and even the royal family of Chybisa.

### BUJOC HUNTER: [FFF 206-3] Kit'tiara Wampusha

The last member to join the group, Kit'tiara was raised by the Bujoc, but only recently discovered that she was not born among them. She joined the group during their time in the Anadel Hills, serving them as a guide and eventually deciding to stay with them rather than return to the Bujoc. A pragmatic person, she has quickly overcome the Bujoc aversion to using metal weapons. Already an expert tracker and hunter, Kit'tiara has become a passable warrior under the tutelage of her companions.

### WOLFHOUNDS: Torga and Kang

The wolfhounds Grimhilda brought with her from Stafen have earned the respect of the group. The dogs have alerted them to danger, found game and aided in battle. Brynn made Kang a studded collar after he saved him from a bear. The dogs will only obey commands from Grimhilda but are fiercely protective of Ygwelve and the other members of their "pack". The one exception is Gaeffel; they really do not like Gaeffel. The pair has had one litter of pups, all of which were given to Chybisan nobles.
