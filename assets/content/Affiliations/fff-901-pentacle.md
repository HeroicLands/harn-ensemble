This eclectic group of individuals represents the middle and lower levels of Harnic society. On the surface they have little in common with each other, and most folk would be surprised to see any three of them together. But most people have not seen what these quick-thinking and resourceful men have seen. In every case, the individual faced grave peril and was warned or actively rescued by Merrimam or one of his agents. Merrimam calls this core group his Pentacle, and takes measures to ensure their continued well being. In return, the members of the pentacle remain ready to respond to Merrimam's call on the shortest possible notice.

## MEMBERS

### THE LEADER: [FFF 303-1] Merrimam Grimwul

An astrologer and arcanist, Merrimam has a vast knowledge of things past, present and even future. An explorer and intellectual, he is intensely curious about everything around him and loves a good challenge or mystery. He is uniquely connected to all levels of society, sought out by nobles, guildsmen and common folk alike. His extensive investments allow him an affluent lifestyle and the time to indulge his curiosity.

### THE SERVANT: [FFF 104-1] Anax of Junius

An ex-gladiator, ex-slave, gardener, manservant and Merrimam's loyal freedman and bodyguard, Anax has long been a careful observer of the human condition. Merrimam often relies on Anax's intuitions about strangers. Normally gentle, calm and placid, Anax can erupt into a fierce and deadly fighter if his master is threatened. Thoroughly grounded in the present, he looks out for his sometimes overly intellectual and distracted master.

### THE 'DOCTOR': [FFF 308-1] Gravith of Dray

A guilded embalmer, Gravith is morbidly fascinated with death in all its forms. An accomplished anatomist, he knows more about the human body and its frailties than most physicians. Merrimam depends on this knowledge injuries, wounds and poisons to reveal the causes of unexplained deaths. Only the members of the Pentacle see beyond Gravith's frightening appearance and fearsome reputation. They are his only real friends, having earned his loyalty and trust.

### THE FERRET: [FFF 408-1] Ryke 'Fat' Hamm

An eccentric herdsman, herbalist and expert tracker, Fat's talent is ferreting out information. As a simple herdsman he can move easily among the serfs and farmers in the countryside and as a poor beggar he can move effortlessly among the urban poor and street people in the towns. Where Merrimam would stand out and be treated with polite silence, Fat can move effortlessly and extract the smallest hint or rumour without suspicion. Ryke is Merrimam's eyes and ears on the street.

### THE MUSCLE: [FFF 328-1] Paguris of Speh AKA 'Monk'

An unlettered sailor, Monk has a nose for trouble and a quick knife to silence it. He has an uncanny memory for people and places, and he knows nearly as much about the stars of the night sky as Merrimam does. A member in good standing of the Seaman's Guild and former thief, he knows the docks well. As Chief Bosun on Merrimam's ship, the Crynto, Monk is the man Merrimam calls on when he needs a little discrete muscle to get things done.

### THE FIXER: [FFF 332-2] Carex of Speh AKA 'Cat'

A well-known and respected businessman, Carex is a thatcher and roofer by day and a second-storey man and ranking member of the Lia-Kavair by night. Mostly retired from burglary now, he still has the skills and the connections to open doors that would otherwise be closed to the group, both literally and metaphorically. His trust and faith in astrology is common knowledge and he is one of Merrimam's best customers. They often take meals together and it is rumoured the Astrologer is a silent partner in Carex's roofing business. His older brother, Paguris (above), stays with him when he is in port.
